Try it: one learning step

Our toy predictor multiplies an input by one adjustable weight. The example input is 2 and the target is 6. Predict what will happen before changing the weight.

Training step: add 0.1 times the input times the prediction error to the weight. This reduces half the squared error for this example. This is a one-parameter teaching model, not an LLM.

Your weekly rhythm

Session 1: lessons 1–2 and the experiment. Session 2: lessons 3–4 and a Python refresher. Session 3: lessons 5–6 and the case study. Start the next week by explaining training, inference, and VSR without looking at your notes. Use spare time to repeat difficult examples.

The course ahead

Only Unit 1 is implemented. Future units will cover Python and mathematics refreshers; neural networks; data sourcing; transformers and LLM families; training and adaptation; inference and hardware; retrieval and agents; speech; image and video generation; VSR; evaluation and production systems.

Your emphasis is understanding and comparison. We will choose the depth of VSR after its introductory lesson. Your planned pace is 3–5 hours weekly, with an optional services budget of up to $100 monthly. This version makes no paid service calls.
