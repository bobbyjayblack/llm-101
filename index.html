Try it: one learning step

Our toy predictor multiplies an input by one adjustable weight. The example input is 2 and the target is 6. Predict what will happen before changing the weight.

Training step: add 0.1 times the input times the prediction error to the weight. This reduces half the squared error for this example. This is a one-parameter teaching model, not an LLM.

13 UNITS · 30 LESSONS
From data to an AI system
Work through foundations, training, data, language models, adaptation, serving, retrieval, speech, images and video, reliability, and a capstone. Each unit includes a runnable local lab with expected results and troubleshooting.


Your weekly rhythm
Plan for 3–5 hours weekly. Read and explain a lesson, predict and run its lab, then compare against the criteria. Return after three days and one week to recall the mechanism using a new example. Take longer on a unit whenever an explanation or result remains unclear.


Course scope
All 13 planned units are available. Labs are small, inspectable exercises; the capstone is a bounded extractive teaching assistant, not a newly trained general-purpose language model. Narration is local AI speech with an optional browser-voice fallback.
The course makes no paid service calls. Notes and reviewed lessons are stored locally. A general conversational tutor and automatic assessment of spoken answers are separate future features.
