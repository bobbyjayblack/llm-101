## Sequence, goals, prerequisites, and assessment

The course is explicitly about LLM foundations and safety. It begins with Transformers and LLM foundations, then proceeds through AI-safety primers, representation engineering, interpretability and model editing, internal Transformer training dynamics, world representations, benchmarks and evaluations, safety/ethics, memorization, watermarking, mechanistic interpretability/formal verification, mathematical reasoning, and LLM security. The sequence ends with project presentations.

The stated prerequisite is CS 182/282A Deep Neural Networks or an equivalent course plus hands-on deep-learning experience. Enrollment is variable-unit. All enrolled students attend in person and submit reading summaries and questions before each lecture. One-unit students write an article summarizing a lecture; students taking more than one unit complete a lab and project, with a written report expected for the two-unit option. The public page therefore documents a reading-and-project seminar rather than a standard exam/weekly-homework course.
