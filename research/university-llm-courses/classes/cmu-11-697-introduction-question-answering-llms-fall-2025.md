## Sequence, goals, prerequisites, and assessment

The sequence starts with QA foundations and a first look at LLM-based QA, then surveys tasks and domains, classic retrieval/NLP pipelines, dataset curation, evaluation and error analysis. It proceeds through sparse and dense retrieval, extractive versus generative QA, RAG and prompt engineering, supervised and parameter-efficient fine-tuning, practical system assembly, and advanced multi-hop, conversational, multimodal, and generative QA. The course is designed as a practical bridge to the advanced project-oriented 11-797 QA course.

The stated goal is to build working knowledge of QA data analysis, solution design, metrics, evaluation, and error analysis. Prerequisites are a statistics/probability course and intermediate Python.

Grading is quizzes 24% (eight at 3% each), homework 66% (five at 10% plus one at 16%), and attendance/class participation 10%. The homework emphasis makes this a useful comparison point for LLM 101’s runnable labs, although the CMU course is more specialized in QA and assumes more Python/statistics preparation.
