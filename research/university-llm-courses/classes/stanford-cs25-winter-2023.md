## Sequence, goals, prerequisites, and assessment

This is a seminar rather than a conventional programming course. The stated objective is to connect ML, NLP, computer vision, biology, speech, reinforcement learning, and other communities around Transformers and to understand their broader implications. The public Winter 2023 schedule starts with Transformer fundamentals, then covers language/human alignment, emergent abilities and scaling in LLMs, computer vision, biology, speech, reinforcement learning, and other applications through instructor and guest-researcher talks.

The Winter 2023 page does not state formal prerequisites. It presents the class as a research seminar and lists attendance responses as the recurring requirement: students submit a response form after every lecture. Recordings were to be published publicly two to four weeks after recording. The current V6 page characterizes weekly attendance as the only homework and makes auditing/livestreaming open to the public; because that is a later version, it is evidence of the seminar’s current public-access model, not a retroactive grading rule for Winter 2023.

Assessment is therefore participation/attendance-oriented, with no public evidence of exams, programming assignments, or a final project for V2. The official schedule is subject to change.

## Book status

No textbook is specified. Those are papers/articles rather than books, so no book dossier is needed for this class.
