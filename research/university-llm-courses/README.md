# University LLM course research

Research date: 2026-09-07. This catalog compares six well-documented university offerings and retains five additional, clearly labeled examples. Official university pages and syllabi establish course identity, sequence, prerequisites, assessment, and reading status. Public student/course feedback is recorded inside each class dossier and kept separate from instructor-review evidence. Historical offerings are dated; no archived page is presented as a current promise.

## Method and limits

Official university sources were preferred for course facts and official author/publisher sources for books. Genuine public student discussions, learner writeups, and instructor-rating pages were searched separately; ratings from unrelated courses are never presented as course evaluations. Absence of a reliable review remains visible in the class dossier. Search results are a bounded public-record sample and cannot estimate overall student satisfaction or teaching quality.

The project comparison and concrete update proposals are in [LLM-101 recommendations](LLM-101-RECOMMENDATIONS.md). The original research pass added documentation; the subsequent research-informed curriculum update changed lesson, lab, and interface behavior as described in the main repository's verification record.

The independent source and coverage review is recorded in [EVIDENCE-AUDIT](EVIDENCE-AUDIT.md). It confirms the edition boundaries, course facts, student-review limits, and the distinction between full-text and preview/TOC chapter reviews.
